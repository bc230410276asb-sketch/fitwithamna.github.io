Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

At Fit With Amna (fitwithamna.github.io), your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent on site, browser type, and device type — collected automatically through analytics tools.
  • Cookies: Small text files stored on your device to improve site functionality and measure traffic.
  • Contact information: If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message.

2. Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC. Google Analytics uses cookies to help us understand how visitors use this site. The data collected is anonymous and does not identify individual users. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Google AdSense

This site participates in Google AdSense, an advertising service by Google LLC. Google AdSense uses cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your browsing history. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to improve user experience. By using this site, you consent to the use of cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

Types of cookies we use:

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics): Track site usage anonymously.
  • Advertising cookies (Google AdSense): Serve relevant advertisements.

5. Third-Party Links

This blog may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

6. Affiliate Links

Some posts on this blog may contain affiliate links. This means we may earn a small commission if you purchase through those links, at no extra cost to you. We only recommend products we genuinely believe in.

7. Children's Privacy

This site is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict our processing of your data
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information (California residents)

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email on our Contact page.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page.
