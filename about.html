Hi, I'm Amna 👋

Fitness blogger · Wellness advocate · Real talk, real results

Welcome to Fit With Amna — a space built for women who want to feel strong, healthy, and confident without extreme diets or overwhelming gym programs.

I started this blog because I struggled for years to find fitness advice that felt realistic. Most content was either too intense or too vague. I wanted something honest — so I created it myself.

Here you'll find workouts for beginners, clean eating guides, yoga routines, and mental wellness tips — all tried and tested by me personally.

What you'll find on this blog

  • 🏋️ Beginner-friendly workout plans
  • 🥗 Simple, nutritious meal ideas
  • 🧘 Yoga and mindfulness guides
  • 💬 Honest wellness advice — no fluff

A note on credentials

I am not a licensed doctor or registered dietitian. Everything I share comes from personal experience, research, and a passion for healthy living. Please read my Disclaimer before acting on any advice here. Always consult a healthcare professional before starting any new diet or exercise program.
